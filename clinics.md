Throughout the year we will be running a new format of bookable Code Clinic sessions where you can discuss problems you're having with your code, ideas for some new code or anything else related to research software. In a session you will meet with research software engineers from across the university who will be able to help you. The bookable sessions will be available once a month on Wednesday afternoons. A sign-up form will be available soon where we'll ask you to provide some details of what you'd like to discuss so we can make sure you can meet with the right experts.

Code Clinic sessions are open to students and staff.
